Robert Raynor

Hi, I’m Robert.

I’m a researcher working on understanding aspects of intelligence and coordination. I draw upon fields like learning theory, computational complexity theory, and statistical physics in seeking to understand what aspects of human thought and coordination will be universal across resource-bounded, agentic systems. See my list of projects for more details.

Some of my roles have included: • Technical lead at EigenLabs, a blockchain startup. • Research asistant and PhD candidate at University of Washington, focused on a mixture of deep learning theory and data marketplace mechanisms. • Applied physicist in the US Air Force, working with advanced optical systems such as high energy lasers, plenoptic cameras, and holographic wavefront sensors.

If you’re interested in talking or collaborating, please reach out at hello @ rayncloud.com.
